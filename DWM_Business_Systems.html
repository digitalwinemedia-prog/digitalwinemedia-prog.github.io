System 03

Monday
Morning Brief

10 minutes every Monday. Open Claude, paste the prompt, get your week in order. No more flying blind.

The master prompt — copy this every Monday
MONDAY PROMPT
Paste into Claude every Monday morning
Run my Monday morning DWM brief. Cover: 1. Revenue this week — invoices raised, what's outstanding, progress toward €200K year target 2. Shoots confirmed this week — dates, clients, kit needed, crew required 3. Deliverables due this week — what needs to be edited and delivered 4. Follow-ups needed — leads gone quiet, pending replies, anyone I should chase 5. One priority this week — the single most important thing to close or complete 6. Anything I've missed — based on what you know about my active projects Keep it punchy. Bullets where possible. No filler.
What each section covers
01
Revenue check
YTD vs target, invoices raised this week, what's outstanding and overdue. Keeps you on track toward €200K without having to open Xero every day.
02
Shoots this week
Everything confirmed in the calendar. Kit to pack, crew to brief, logistics to confirm. No more showing up unprepared.
03
Edit queue
What's sitting in the To Edit pile, what's due this week, what can go to Jack. Keeps deliverables from piling up.
04
Follow-ups
Leads that have gone quiet, proposals that haven't been responded to, clients waiting on something from you.
05
One priority
The single most important thing to close this week. Not a list of ten. One thing. Forces focus.
Additional quick prompts
Revenue check

How am I tracking toward €200K this year? What do I need to earn per month for the rest of the year to hit it?

Invoice chase

Who owes me money right now? Draft a polite but firm chase message for any invoice over 14 days outstanding.

Shoot prep

Build me a one-page shoot prep document for [shoot name] — shot list, call time, kit, crew, logistics.

System 04

Content
Pipeline

Every shoot feeds your socials automatically. One brain dump after a shoot generates a week of content. Here's the formula.

The core formula — after every shoot
INPUT
Brain dump about the shoot — what happened, who was there, what was the energy
→
OUTPUT
5 Instagram captions, 3 TikTok hooks, 1 YouTube script outline
Post-shoot content prompt
AFTER EVERY SHOOT
Paste after your brain dump
Based on that shoot, generate: 1. Five Instagram captions — short, punchy, in my voice. No corporate language. No hashtag spam. One or two max per caption. Vary the angles (behind the scenes, creative take, client praise, culture, personal reflection). 2. Three TikTok hooks — opening lines that stop the scroll. Under 8 words each. Statement, question, or provocative take. 3. One YouTube script outline — structure for a 3-5 minute behind the scenes video. Intro hook, three main beats, CTA at the end. My voice: direct, no filler, edgy, music-driven, premium but not corporate. Think festival aftermovie meets documentary filmmaker.
Weekly content calendar
MONDAY
Plan the week
Pick 3 pieces of content to post this week. Pull from the post-shoot library or generate new from recent brain dumps.
TUESDAY / THURSDAY
Post Instagram reels
Two reels per week minimum. Shoot content, behind the scenes, or client work. Caption from the library.
WEDNESDAY
TikTok — talking head or hook
Record direct to camera using one of the TikTok hooks generated post-shoot. 30-60 seconds. No editing required.
FRIDAY
Authority post
A lesson learned, a behind the scenes thought, a hot take on the industry. Builds credibility beyond the work itself.
MONTHLY
YouTube upload
One behind the scenes video per month. Shot on the day, scripted from the post-shoot prompt, edited simply.
Content type library — prompts for each format
Instagram caption — work post

Write me an Instagram caption for a reel of [shoot/client]. Punchy, no filler. My voice: direct, edgy, music-driven. Max 3 lines. End with one or two relevant hashtags only.

TikTok hook generator

Give me 5 TikTok opening hooks for a video about [topic/shoot]. Under 8 words each. Scroll-stopping. Direct. My voice is confident, not cringe. No emojis in the hook.

YouTube BTS script

Write a YouTube script for a 4-minute behind the scenes video about [shoot]. Hook: [hook]. Cover: the brief, what we shot, the challenge, the outcome. End with a CTA to get in touch.
