01 — WHO WE ARE

THE
VISION.

Digital Wine Media is not a typical video production company. It is a one-person studio built on years of being inside the rooms where culture happens — festival pits, nightclub booths, brand launches, artist sessions — and knowing instinctively how to capture what actually makes those moments feel the way they do.

The output is darker, grittier, and more sinister in energy than anything else being produced in the Irish market. That is not a style choice. It is a point of view that has been earned on location, one shoot at a time.

WHY DWM.

Most production companies shoot events. We live inside them. There is a difference between pointing a camera at a room and actually understanding the culture you are documenting. DWM has earned that understanding over years of being the person in the pit, in the booth, at the shoot.

The output is grittier, darker, more urgent than the standard Irish market produces. That is intentional. It is a niche built for brands that want to show up in culture, not just advertise next to it.
